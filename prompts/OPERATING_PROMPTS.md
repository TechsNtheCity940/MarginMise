# Operating Prompts

## Process invoices
Process all unprocessed invoices. Preserve originals, validate arithmetic, update the workbook, create new Item Master entries, and place uncertainty in Review Queue.

## Record sales
Record the supplied sales period. Preserve supplied net sales and do not subtract tax twice. Recalculate purchase-to-sales metrics.

## Price review
Show products exceeding the price-alert threshold, including previous and current prices, percentage change, vendor, invoice, date, and source.

## Month-end
Confirm invoices and sales, calculate purchases versus sales, calculate COGS when inventory values exist, disclose open reviews, and export a manager report.

## Duplicate audit
Find possible duplicate invoices without deleting anything automatically.
