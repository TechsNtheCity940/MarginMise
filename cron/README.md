# Suggested Scheduled Jobs

These are not automatically installed because storage locations, reporting times, and delivery targets vary.

- Invoice intake: every 15 minutes
- Nightly workbook validation: 2:00 AM local time
- Weekly manager summary: Monday at 7:00 AM
- Month-end report: first day of the month at 7:00 AM

Confirm folder locations, workbook ID, delivery target, timezone, and review policy before creating jobs.
